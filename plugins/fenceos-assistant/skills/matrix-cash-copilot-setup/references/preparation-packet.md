# Useful work when inputs are incomplete

Deliver this instead of a vague request to gather documents. Preserve work toward the agreed next-year annual budget.

1. A source inventory: available file/source, business, date/period, reliability and what it establishes.
2. A provisional budget outline with supported values, approved planning assumptions and clearly empty required sections. Do not call inferred statement categories reconciled accounting.
3. A short list of decisions the owner can make now and dependent steps that must wait.
4. A ready-to-copy bookkeeper request, adapted to the missing items only:

"I'm preparing our budget for [exact period]. Please share the latest reliable income-and-expense report by month for [useful periods], showing its cash/accrual basis and any unfinished months. For the cash plan, I also need reconciled available cash as of [date], where we track unpaid bills and expected collections, and upcoming payroll, employer costs and debt payment dates. [Specific unresolved classification] needs your clarification. Please flag what is still provisional and when it is expected to be available. These records will let us finish [specific sections]."

Do not include items already resolved or assume a delivery date. Provide the text in chat/private file; sending to a named bookkeeper requires separate authorization. Do not change books or payroll systems. Keep the records owner and request status in the checkpoint and avoid repeating questions on resume.
